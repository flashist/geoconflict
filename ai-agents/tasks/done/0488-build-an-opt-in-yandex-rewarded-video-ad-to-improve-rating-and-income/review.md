## Accepted residuals (shared, do-not-re-litigate)
- **R1 — the 60 s no-interstitial gap is page-only** — What: `lastRewardedVideoClosedAtMs` stays module memory in `FlashistFacade.ts` (`let lastRewardedVideoClosedAtMs`); the gap protects every `showInterstitial()` call on the match page (WinModal Exit/Keep/Spectate, `leaveMatch`), and is forgotten on Exit's full page load, so the next page's lobby/start interstitial (`PublicLobby`, `HostLobbyModal`, `SinglePlayerModal`, `Main`) may show within 60 s of a rewarded video. · Why (structural): carrying it across the navigation needs a persisted store (e.g. `sessionStorage`), a new storage surface for a cost of at most one early interstitial per watch, with no XP or money effect; Yandex already controls interstitial frequency itself and may decline some (unchecked). Owner ruling 2026-10-24, `AskUserQuestion` in the fkit-lead session, "R1: should the 60 s ad gap survive the page reload after Exit?" → "No, page-only" — "Keep as is; record it as an accepted gap (Yandex's own ad limits may block some anyway, unchecked)." Rejected: a per-tab store surviving same-tab navigation (the reviewer's example). · Re-raise only if: Yandex moderation or the verify task's live run flags back-to-back ads after a rewarded video, or Exit stops being a full page load (then re-check the gap still holds), or the owner asks for the gap to survive navigation.
- **R2 — the sidebar/settings exit does not wait for a running bonus claim** — What: only WinModal's `_handleExit` waits (≤ 5 s, `EXIT_WAIT_FOR_REWARDED_CLAIM_MS`) for `this.rewardedClaim`; `leaveMatch()` in `src/client/MatchExit.ts` navigates after its interstitial without waiting, so a claim still in its first try or a retry wait can be cut off and the 1 XP lost, with no retry on the next load. · Why (structural): the window needs a watch, then Spectate, then a second exit path inside the claim's few seconds; the loss is at most 1 XP for that match, and only when the request had not reached the profile box. Closing it means threading the WinModal's claim into `MatchExit`, a new coupling for a rare 1-XP case. Owner ruling 2026-10-24, `AskUserQuestion` in the fkit-lead session, "R2: should the sidebar/settings exit also wait for a running bonus claim?" → "No, accept it" — "Rare, 1 XP; record it as an accepted gap." Rejected: the same 5 s wait in `leaveMatch` (the reviewer's suggestion). · Re-raise only if: the bonus grows beyond 1 XP per match, the claim gets a next-load retry queue (then decide both together), or live analytics show a material share of `Ad:Rewarded:Watched` with no `Ad:Rewarded:Reward:*` event.
